Data Description
You are a risk analyst working in a financial organisation that offers credit cards to customers. Analyze the customer case who has defaulted using the given data. 
The attached spend data comprises of the spends made by the customer via his credit card in last 18 months before their default. Also, the payments/ returns data showcases the payments made by the customer towards his credit card in last 1 year before the customer defaulted on his business credit card. Sharing description wrt all data headers -
1. Date: Transaction Date
2. Month: Transaction Month
3. Amount: Spend Amount (in USD)
4. Merchant Name: Name of the merchant where spend was made
5. Merchant Industry: Merchant Industry basis SIC4 code. Some rows might be empty, you can search the industry types for them basis your knowledge or online resources.
6. Merchant Risk Score: Risk Score of Merchant. Values above 0.7 are risky
7. Spend Concentration: One way spend concentration risk rate of a customer at merchants in last 30 days. Values above 2.4 are considered risky.
8. RNN Spend Score: RNN risk score. Values above 0.017 are considered risky.
9. Spend Divergence Index: Index for Spend divergence. Values below 1 are considered risky.
10. Customer Industry: The industry in which the customer's business operates
11. Payment Date: Date on which payment was made 
12. Payment Amount: Payment Amount (in USD) 
13. Payment Bank Account: Bank Account Number from which payment was made (masked to 0,1,2 etc for security purposes) 
14. Return Flag: Indicates whether the payment made was received by the company or not. 1 indicates that the payment was not received while 0 means that the payment was received. 
15. Return Reason: Reason for the returned payment (where Return Flag was 1)
