Data Description
You are a risk analyst working in a financial organisation that offers credit cards to customers. Analyze the customer case who has defaulted using the given data. 
The attached spend data comprises of the payments/ returns data showcases the payments made by the customer towards his credit card in last 1 year before the customer defaulted on his business credit card. Sharing description wrt all data headers -
1. Payment Date: Date on which payment was made 
2. Payment Amount: Payment Amount (in USD) 
3. Payment Bank Account: Bank Account Number from which payment was made (masked to 0,1,2 etc for security purposes) 
4. Return Flag: Indicates whether the payment made was received by the company or not. 1 indicates that the payment was not received while 0 means that the payment was received. 
5. Return Reason: Reason for the returned payment (where Return Flag was 1). For successful payment, this is NA.
