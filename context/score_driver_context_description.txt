Data Description
You are a risk analyst working in a financial organisation that offers credit cards to customers. Analyze the customer case who has defaulted using the given data. 
The company built XGBoost model to predict the credit card default probability internally (credit_loss_prob) or both internally ad externally (tot_struct_risk_score). The attached data showcases the monthly top/ bottom score drivers of the credit_loss_prob (top_cdss & bottom_cdss) and top/ bottom score drivers for tot_struct_risk_score (top_tsr and bottom_tsr). Top drivers are some variables that eleate the default probability and bottom drivers are some variables that suppress the default probability. The data is given for 18 months before the customer defaulted on his business credit card. Sharing description wrt all data headers and their risk thresholds –
